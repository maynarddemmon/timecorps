HSU, Beatrix Lan — Forensic Attestation Specialist, Archive Division (ID: BH)

Hsu was born in 2143 in the Taipei Highlands and trained as a forensic scientist, working for the Pacific Accord's investigation service on cases of fraud and forgery. She joined the Corps at twenty-eight, recruited by Archive Division to investigate events where the historical record had been deliberately falsified.

She is the Corps' leading forensic specialist. Hsu can determine whether a document is genuine, whether a witness is lying and whether an event happened the way the record claims, often within minutes of arriving on site. Her attestation scores are the highest in the Corps. Her jump efficiency is below average, and she deploys only when the truth matters more than the timing.

The pink monocle lens over her left eye is a forensic scanner that reads chemical traces, stress patterns and microscopic wear. Period witnesses assume it is a jeweler's loupe or an eccentric affectation, and she has perfected the manner of a woman too absorbed in her work to notice their stares.

Hsu is precise, skeptical and exacting. She distrusts any account she has not verified for herself, including those of other agents, and she has corrected the official record of several Corps missions. Her colleagues respect her enormously and are careful about what they put in their reports when she is on the review panel.
