NOVAK, Ansel Tomas — Senior Instructor, Nexus Academy (ID: AN)

Novak was born in 2118 in the Prague Terraces and is the oldest agent still certified for field duty. He was among the founding cohort of the Time Corps and carried out some of its earliest deployments, in the years when agents entered the past with little more than a jump coordinate and a period costume. When the academy opened, he became one of its first instructors, and he has trained most of the senior agents now on the roster.

He rarely deploys any more. Command calls on him when an event is so delicate that experience matters more than speed, and when it does, Novak is careful, thorough and slow. His attestation work remains among the best in the Corps. The paradox load on his file is a legacy of the early years, and the medical wing reviews it before every deployment he makes.

Novak cultivates the appearance of a well-dressed elder in any period, which makes him nearly invisible. Nobody suspects a grey-bearded gentleman in tinted spectacles of anything more than wandering into the wrong room. He says that the best cover an agent can have is to look like someone other people have already decided not to notice.

He is gentle, exacting and dryly funny, and his former students speak of him with a mixture of affection and dread. His final lecture to every graduating class is always the same: the past owes you nothing, and you owe it everything, so tread accordingly.
