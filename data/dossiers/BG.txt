GALE, Bryony Fern — Chronal Sensor Specialist, Survey Division (ID: BG)

Gale was born in 2144 in the Welsh Valleys Reserve and trained as an acoustic engineer, designing listening arrays for the seismic monitoring networks that watch the unstable coastlines. The Corps recruited her to build instruments that could detect paradox before it became dangerous, and she ended up wearing the first one she designed.

The ring-shaped device fitted over her right ear is a chronal sensor that translates disturbances in the field into sound. Gale hears paradox as it forms, and she is Survey Division's specialist in detecting instability before it spreads. Her attestation work is exceptional, her chronal efficiency sound, and the small paradox load on her file comes from deliberately testing the sensor near an unstable event.

On deployment she moves slowly and listens constantly, often with her head tilted toward sounds that no one else can hear. Period witnesses sometimes take her for someone listening to music, which in a sense she is. She says that a healthy event hums, and a dying one screams.

Gale is dreamy, intense and deeply sensitive to her surroundings. She finds crowded events exhausting and quiet ones beautiful, and she spends her rest days in the Nexus listening to recordings of the chronal field. Her colleagues find her strange and indispensable in roughly equal measure.
