XU, Alina Mei — Field Agent, Recovery Division (ID: AX)

Xu was born in 2141 in the Shanghai Spires, the towers built above the drowned city during the Retreat. She joined the Corps at twenty-two as a navigator and spent six years plotting jump solutions in Nexus Operations before transferring to Recovery Division, where she believed she could do more good.

Her career was nearly ended in 2169 by a jump failure that trapped her in a collapsing chronal field for eleven seconds. The right side of her face and skull were rebuilt with cybernetic plating, and the implants that replaced her damaged nerves now let her sense chronal gradients directly. Her chronal efficiency has been exceptional ever since. Her investigative work has suffered, and she admits she finds it harder to concentrate on what is in front of her than on what is moving around her. The paradox load on her file dates from the accident.

Xu makes no effort to hide the implant, and in most periods she simply claims to have been injured in an industrial accident, which is close enough to the truth. Colleagues find her unflinching directness about her injuries disarming, and occasionally uncomfortable.

She is focused, fearless and fiercely committed to Recovery's mission of bringing people home. She has volunteered for every recovery involving an agent trapped by a jump failure, and she has asked Command to assign her to the next one before it happens.
