LOWE, Abram Sterling — Field Agent, Containment Division (ID: AL)

Lowe was born in 2134 in the Chicago Lakewall, the fortified shoreline that protects the city from the storms off the swollen lake. He worked as a structural inspector on the wall for nearly a decade, climbing its maintenance shafts in weather that closed every other job in the city. The Corps recruited him for his nerve, and for a report he filed that predicted a section failure three weeks before it happened.

In Containment Division he has become the agent sent to hold the line. Lowe is large, deliberate and immovable, and he is at his best in the final minutes of a collapsing event, when the task is simply to stay put and keep others safe until recall. His chronal efficiency is adequate and his investigative work unremarkable. His record of bringing every assigned agent home is spotless.

Lowe dresses plainly on assignment and relies on his size to deter trouble before it starts. He carries himself like a man who expects to be obeyed, and in most periods he is. Period witnesses describe him as a policeman, a foreman or a bodyguard, which is usually close enough to his actual role that he has never needed to correct them.

He is taciturn and unfailingly polite, and slow to anger to a degree that his colleagues find almost unnerving. He has only one strong opinion about his work, which he gives to every new partner: a containment is a failure that has not happened yet, and his job is to keep it that way for as long as it takes.
