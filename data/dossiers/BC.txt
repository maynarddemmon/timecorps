CASTELLANOS, Bree Inés — Field Agent, Containment Division (ID: BC)

Castellanos was born in 2142 in the Buenos Aires Riverlands and trained as a munitions disposal engineer, clearing unexploded ordnance from the conflict zones of the Retreat years. She is one of the few agents in the Corps who understands explosives, and Containment recruited her at twenty-seven for events where something is about to go off.

She is the division's specialist in industrial and military accidents: fires, explosions, boiler failures and the cargo nobody declared. Castellanos can read a ship's hold, a factory floor or an ammunition store the way other agents read a room, and she knows what will happen, and when, if nobody intervenes. Her chronal efficiency and attestation scores are both solid.

On deployment she works close to the machinery, often in cramped and dangerous spaces, and she carries a period sidearm and a set of tools she refuses to discuss. She has been deployed to more explosions than any other agent in the Corps, and she has walked away from all of them.

Castellanos is calm, careful and slightly fatalistic. She treats every deployment as a disposal operation, and she believes that most disasters are not accidents but decisions made long before the moment of catastrophe. She prefers to be sent to the decision rather than the explosion, and she has asked Command, repeatedly, to let her.
