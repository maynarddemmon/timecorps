QUILL, Anya Rosalind — Intelligence Analyst, Archive Division (ID: AQ)

Quill was born in 2142 in the Vancouver Heights, the city that climbed the mountains when the sea came in. She studied information systems and spent six years as an intelligence analyst for the Pacific Accord, tracking smuggling routes across the new coastlines. The Corps recruited her to build its first comprehensive index of historical communications, and she deployed for the first time only after the index was finished.

She is the Corps' specialist in intelligence history: codes, telegrams, wireless traffic, diplomatic dispatches and the ways in which information moves, or fails to move, between the people who need it. Her attestation work is excellent and her jump efficiency average. She is most often deployed to events where a message was sent but never read, or read and never believed.

On assignment Quill favors severe tailoring, tinted glasses and a notebook she carries in every period. She writes down everything, in a private shorthand of her own design that the Corps cryptographers have been unable to break and she has declined to explain.

She is sharp, impatient and exceptionally good at her work. She has no tolerance for vague briefings and a reputation for rewriting mission plans that arrive on her desk with gaps in them. Her colleagues say that if a warning was ever lost in the past, Quill can tell you where it went, who dropped it and exactly how long it sat there.
