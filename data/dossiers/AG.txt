GARZA, Aurelio Benedicto — Chronal Navigator, Nexus Operations (ID: AG)

Garza was born in 2136 in Monterrey Nuevo, the desert city rebuilt around solar foundries during the Retreat. He trained as a high-altitude transport pilot and flew cargo over the Sierra Madre for eight years before an accident grounded him. A Corps recruiter who had read his flight records offered him a different kind of navigation: plotting the paths agents take through the chronal field.

Navigators do not usually deploy. They work from the Nexus, computing jump solutions and steering agents around unstable regions of the field. Garza was the first navigator certified for field duty, after his solutions proved so efficient that Command wanted him carrying them into events himself. His chronogation scores are among the best ever recorded. His investigation scores are not, and he freely admits that he would rather be moving through an event than studying it.

The visor he wears on deployment is a navigation display wired directly into the chronal field. It lets him see jump windows as they open and close, and it makes him look, as one colleague put it, like a man permanently waiting for a green light. Period witnesses take it for eccentric eyewear, or for a medical device, which in a sense it has become.

Garza is steady, methodical and quietly competitive. He tracks his own jump efficiency the way he once tracked fuel burn, and he has never quite forgiven the one deployment in his career when he arrived three minutes late.
