ORME, Alden Crispin — Archivist Agent, Archive Division (ID: AO)

Orme was born in 2147 in the Cambridge Fens Reserve, where his family maintained the drowned-library recovery project that spent two decades lifting books out of the flooded colleges. He learned to read in several dead languages before he learned to swim, and he joined the Corps' Archive Division straight from the recovery project at twenty-one.

He is a document specialist. Orme reconstructs the paper trail of an event, finding and reading the letters, logs, manifests and minutes that record what people decided and when. His attestation scores are among the highest in the Corps. His jump efficiency is the lowest on the active roster, and he is deployed only when the record matters more than the timing.

On assignment he dresses like a clerk and behaves like one, with his eyes on the page and his thoughts elsewhere. Period witnesses almost never remember him, and he considers this his greatest professional achievement. His handlers have learned that when Orme looks up from a document, he has found something worth reporting.

He is shy, meticulous and quietly obstinate. He has a strong distaste for interventions that alter documents, and has twice filed formal objections when a mission plan called for it. Colleagues say that arguing with Orme about the historical record is like arguing with the tide, and that he is usually right, which only makes it worse.
