ZELLER, Anika Rosa — Reconstruction Specialist, Archive Division (ID: AZ)

Zeller was born in 2146 in the Hamburg Floating Quarters, the city that learned to rise and fall with the tide. She trained as a forensic archaeologist and spent five years excavating the flooded districts of the old North Sea ports, reconstructing lives from what the water left behind. Archive Division recruited her to do the same thing in the past.

She reconstructs events from their traces. Zeller can walk through an event, notice what is out of place, and piece together what must have happened from the smallest details: a misplaced chair, a torn page, a door left open. Her attestation scores are among the highest in the Corps. Her jump efficiency is poor, and her handlers budget generously for her.

On assignment she is unremarkable, dressed plainly and moving slowly. She looks, as one colleague put it, like someone who has lost an earring and is too polite to ask for help finding it. Period witnesses rarely notice her, and those who do are usually happy to help.

Zeller is curious, meticulous and relentlessly cheerful. She treats every event as an excavation, and she is never happier than when the evidence contradicts the official account. She has a small collection of objects she has been permitted to bring back from the past, all of them ordinary and none of them valuable, and she says each one taught her something no document could.
