REYES, Ayumi Clara — Jump Pilot, Recovery Division (ID: AR)

Reyes was born in 2148 in New Hokkaido, the northern settlements that took in climate refugees from across the Pacific. Her mother was a Filipino relief pilot and her father a Japanese fisherman, and she grew up flying supply drones across the island chains before she was old enough to hold a license. The Corps found her through a drone racing circuit, where she had never lost a race in bad weather.

In Recovery Division she flies jumps the way she flew drones: fast, low and close to the edge of what the equipment can do. Her chronal efficiency is one of the best in the division, and she is the agent Recovery sends when a window is closing and someone has to get in and out before it shuts. Her investigative work is adequate, and she rarely stays long enough to use it.

Reyes wears a red navigation visor on deployment, patched into her jump rig, and a red coat that she insists is lucky. Period witnesses notice both, which her handlers dislike. She points out that nobody ever remembers the face of a woman in a red coat, only the coat.

She is quick-tongued, fearless and restless between missions. She has recovered nine agents from collapsing events and lost none. She says she intends to keep that record, and her colleagues believe her.
