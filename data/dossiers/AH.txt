HARROW, Axel Brennan — Field Agent, Containment Division (ID: AH)

Harrow was born in 2141 in the Rotterdam Polders, a city that survived the Retreat by learning to live below the sea. He served six years in the Polder Guard, the civil force that patrols the dikes and pumping stations, before the Corps recruited him for its Containment Division, the section responsible for sealing off events that have begun to unravel.

Containment agents go where paradox is already loose. They stabilize event boundaries, extract anyone at risk and hold the line until an unstable event settles or collapses. Harrow is well suited to the work. He is fast, decisive and comfortable acting before he has the full picture, and his after-action reports read like dike inspections: what failed, where, and how much time was left.

The paradox load on his file comes from a containment operation in 1848 Paris, where he was forced to re-enter a collapsing event to recover a survey agent who had missed recall. He recovered her. He does not regard the load as a mark against him, and his superiors agree.

Harrow is short-spoken and dry, with a pale, watchful composure that colleagues find either reassuring or unnerving depending on the situation. He carries a period sidearm on deployment and has drawn it rarely. He says the point of carrying it is to make it unnecessary, which is also how he thinks about the rest of Containment.
