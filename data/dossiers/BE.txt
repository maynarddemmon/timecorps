ESTEVES, Beatriz Helena — Field Medic, Recovery Division (ID: BE)

Esteves was born in 2144 in the Lisbon Heights and trained as a trauma surgeon. She spent six years in the field hospitals of the Atlantic coast, treating the injured from storm surges and seawall failures, before joining the Corps at thirty. Recovery Division recruited her because agents extracted from collapsing events often need medical care before they can be moved.

She is one of only three field medics in the Corps. Esteves deploys with extraction teams, stabilizing injured agents and historical figures long enough to get them home. She is fast, steady and very good under pressure, and she has saved more lives in the past than any other agent on the roster. Her chronal efficiency is sound and her investigative work adequate.

On deployment she dresses for whatever period she is in and carries a medical kit disguised to match it. She has performed surgery in a Napoleonic field tent, a Victorian railway carriage and the hold of a sinking ship, and she says the hardest part is always the lighting.

Esteves is brisk, practical and unexpectedly funny. She has a medic's disregard for rank and a surgeon's impatience with people who waste her time. Her colleagues say she is the person you most want to see when you wake up hurt in the past, and the last person you want to find you doing something stupid.
