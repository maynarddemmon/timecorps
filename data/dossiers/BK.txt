KAUR, Beatrice Simran — Oversight Agent, Office of the Inspector (ID: BK)

Kaur was born in 2141 in the Delhi Highlands and trained as a lawyer, specializing in the investigation of official misconduct. She spent six years with the Pacific Accord's inspectorate before joining the Corps at twenty-nine, recruited by the Office of the Inspector, the small section that investigates the Corps itself.

She deploys to audit other agents' work: to confirm that interventions were carried out as reported, that paradox was incurred as recorded, and that no agent has altered history for their own purposes. Kaur's attestation work is excellent and her chronal efficiency sound. Her presence on a mission is rarely announced in advance, and many agents never learn that she was there.

On deployment she keeps to the shadows and carries a period sidearm, which she has never drawn. She dresses to blend in and watches everything, and her reports are notorious for their thoroughness and their lack of mercy.

Kaur is cool, correct and entirely incorruptible. She is not popular among field agents, and she does not try to be. She believes that an organization with the power to change history must be held to a higher standard than any other, and she regards her work as the only thing standing between the Corps and the temptation to use that power for itself.
