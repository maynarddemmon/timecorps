DUNNE, Brigid Aoife — Deep Placement Agent, Time Corps (ID: BD)

Dunne was born in 2145 in Cork Uplands, not far from the ruins of the old Queenstown harbor. Her grandmother kept the family's stories of the town's great liners and its emigrant ships, and Dunne grew up knowing more about the port's history than the historians who studied it. She joined the Corps at twenty-three, and Deep Placement recruited her for her command of the Irish coast in the early twentieth century.

She specializes in long-term placements in Ireland and along the Irish Sea. Dunne has completed three, including a seven-month placement in Queenstown in 1914 and 1915, and she knows the town's harbor, its people and its politics intimately. Her attestation work is strong and her jump efficiency average.

She is soft-spoken and easy to talk to, and period witnesses confide in her readily. She wears her hair loose and her clothes plain, and in any Irish town she looks exactly like someone's cousin home for a visit. Her handlers say that she can walk into any public house on the coast and walk out an hour later with the story of every family in it.

Dunne is warm, perceptive and quietly determined. She has a deep attachment to the harbor her grandmother remembered, and she has asked to be assigned to any operation that touches it. Command has noted the request, and noted also that it will need to watch her closely if she ever gets one.
