FENWICK, Ambrose Hale — Field Agent, Recovery Division (ID: AF)

Fenwick was born in 2149 in the London Barrier Towns, the high-rise settlements that line the Thames flood defenses. He grew up in a family of theater costumers and spent his teenage years dressing actors for historical productions, which gave him an encyclopedic knowledge of period clothing and the manners that went with it. He applied to the Nexus academy at twenty on a dare and was accepted on the strength of an entrance essay about the buttonholes of nineteenth-century waistcoats.

He is one of the youngest agents in Recovery Division and one of its most promising. His chronal efficiency is well above average, and he is unusually good at entering an event already dressed, spoken and behaving correctly for it. His investigative work is merely adequate. Evaluators note that he tends to see how a room looks before he sees what is happening in it.

Fenwick carries himself like a man who has been dressing for the part all his life, because he has. He is meticulous about his tailoring in every period, and he is never without a period-correct sidearm on recovery assignments, where the people he is sent to retrieve are rarely the only ones looking for them.

Colleagues find him charming, a little vain and far braver than he looks. His handlers have learned to trust his instincts about when to leave an event, which are rarely wrong, and to double-check his reports about why, which are occasionally more elegant than accurate.
