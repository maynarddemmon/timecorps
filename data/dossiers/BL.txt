LINDQVIST, Bo Astrid — Field Agent (Medical Hold), Time Corps (ID: BL)

Lindqvist was born in 2145 in the Gothenburg Archipelago and joined the Corps at twenty-two after a career as a competitive free diver. She was assigned to Intervention Division and rose quickly, completing more than thirty deployments in her first five years and earning a reputation as one of the most versatile agents in the Corps.

She is currently on medical hold. Lindqvist's paradox load reached the Corps' safe limit during a series of back-to-back interventions in 2173, when she was sent repeatedly into the same cluster of events to correct a cascading failure. She succeeded, but at a cost the medical wing is still assessing. She has not been cleared to deploy since.

Paradox saturation affects agents differently. For Lindqvist, it has brought fragmented memories of events that did not happen and moments when she loses track of which version of the past she is in. She wears an armored gauntlet on her right hand, fitted to suppress the tremors that began after her last deployment, and she often closes her eyes when she speaks, as though listening for something.

Lindqvist is determined, frustrated and fiercely insistent that she is ready to return to duty. Her doctors disagree. Command has kept her on the roster in the hope that she will recover, and her file carries a standing instruction: she is not to be deployed to any event she has already visited, under any circumstances.
