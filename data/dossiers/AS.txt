SATO, Ava Mireille — Field Agent, Intervention Division (ID: AS)

Sato was born in 2145 in the Kyoto Highlands, where her family ran a cultural preservation trust that restored the city's temples after the storms of the Retreat. She grew up among diplomats, donors and scholars, and learned to charm all three by the time she was ten. She studied languages at the Geneva Arcology and joined the Corps at twenty-four, recruited by Lucian Gordon of Intervention Division, who had noticed her at a treaty conference.

She works in the salons, ballrooms and dining rooms of history, the places where influence moves quietly between people who never write anything down. Sato speaks nine languages fluently and several more convincingly, and she has an unusual gift for being remembered as someone other than herself. Her attestation work is strong and her jump efficiency sound.

She wears tinted glasses in every period, a habit borrowed from Gordon that she has refined into something entirely her own. She adjusts them as she decides what to say, and colleagues have learned to read the gesture as a warning.

Sato is gracious, perceptive and ruthless about outcomes. She believes that most history turns on a few private conversations, and that an agent in the right room at the right moment can do more than an army. Her reports are elegant, discreet and occasionally leave out names that she considers nobody else's business.
