THORNE, Amara Isolde — Deep Placement Agent, Time Corps (ID: AT)

Thorne was born in 2139 in the Cornish Highlands, one of the small, self-sufficient communities that survived the Retreat by keeping to themselves. She grew up in a family of stonemasons and learned early that the most useful thing a stranger can be is unremarkable. She entered the Nexus academy at twenty-three and was assigned to Deep Placement before she graduated.

She is one of the division's most successful long-term agents. Thorne has completed five placements, the longest lasting fourteen months as a domestic servant in a 1906 London household at the center of a political scandal. She is patient, observant and almost invisible, and her reports are dense with the small details of daily life that other agents overlook. The paradox load on her file comes from that long placement, when she crossed paths with a version of herself on a return visit.

Thorne rarely looks directly at anyone, a habit she cultivated in service and has never shed. She wears her hair loose across her face and speaks softly, and colleagues sometimes forget she is in the room. She considers this a professional asset and is quietly amused by it.

She is private, dryly humorous and deeply attached to the people she has lived among in the past. Her handlers note that she grieves for them after every placement, and that she always asks to know what became of them. The Corps tells her when it can.
