YOSHIDA, Amaya Noriko — Field Agent, Containment Division (ID: AY)

Yoshida was born in 2138 in the Osaka Hills and served eight years in the Kansai Disaster Response Force before joining the Corps at twenty-nine. She came to Containment Division already expert in managing collapse, and she found the work of sealing unstable events only a little stranger than the work she had left.

She is among the division's most experienced agents, and one of very few to have faced a Chronovore manifestation and remained on active duty. The red scarring across her face and neck is a chronal burn from that encounter, in 1871 Chicago, where she held an event boundary while two survey agents recalled. The paradox load on her file dates from the same containment, and the medical wing monitors it closely.

Yoshida is calm, deliberate and entirely unafraid of the things other agents fear most. She has become the division's specialist in paradox containment, and she is the agent Command assigns when an event shows the early signs of a manifestation. She moves carefully, speaks quietly and does not waste time on anything that will not help her hold the line.

She is also a patient and generous teacher, and she spends much of her time between deployments training new Containment agents. Her one rule, which she repeats to every class, is that the Chronovores are not an enemy to be fought but a consequence to be avoided, and that the only way to survive them is never to give them a reason to come.
