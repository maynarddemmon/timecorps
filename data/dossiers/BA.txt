ASANO, Blythe Emiko — Team Leader, Recovery Division (ID: BA)

Asano was born in 2140 in the Seattle Terraces, where her family ran a search and rescue outfit working the landslide country of the Cascades. She joined the Corps at twenty-four and has spent her entire career in Recovery Division, where she now leads one of its four extraction teams.

She is the division's most decorated team leader. Asano plans recoveries with the precision of a military operation and executes them with the flexibility of a street fight, and she has a gift for turning a failed extraction into a successful one in the space of a few seconds. Her chronal efficiency is excellent and her investigative work solid.

On deployment she wears a red tactical visor and carries a period sidearm, and she has made both look natural in every era from the Napoleonic Wars onward. Her team members say she can make a borrowed coat look like a uniform, and that period witnesses tend to follow her orders without quite knowing why.

Asano is decisive, demanding and fiercely loyal to her team. She has never left an agent behind, and she regards the idea as unthinkable rather than merely forbidden. Her colleagues in other divisions find her brusque. Her own team would follow her into a collapsing event without hesitation, and several of them have.
