JANSEN, Asher Pim — Field Agent, Survey Division (ID: AJ)

Jansen was born in 2150 in the orbital habitat Meridian Ring, the same station that produced Nexus Operations technician Adrian Pierce. He was raised by station engineers and spent most of his childhood in low gravity, which left him with an unnatural stillness that period witnesses sometimes find unsettling. He came to Earth for the first time at eighteen to sit the Nexus academy entrance examinations, and passed them with the highest spatial reasoning scores of his year.

He was assigned to Survey Division, which maps events before any intervention is attempted. Survey agents enter an event, observe it, measure it and leave without changing anything. Jansen is exceptionally good at the first and last parts. His jump efficiency is high and his presence in an event barely registers on the chronal instruments.

The visor he wears is a survey rig that records an event in full spectrum for later analysis. It covers his eyes entirely, and he rarely removes it on deployment. Colleagues joke that nobody at the Nexus is certain what color his eyes are, and he has not settled the question.

Jansen is formal, reserved and precise to the point of literalism. He speaks little and writes a great deal, and his survey reports are models of their kind: complete, dispassionate and free of any opinion about what should be done with the event he has mapped. He considers that someone else's job, and he would prefer to keep it that way.
