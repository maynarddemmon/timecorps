EKLUND, Anton Viggo — Attestation Specialist, Archive Division (ID: AE)

Eklund was born in 2140 in Longyearbyen, the Arctic town that grew around the old seed vault after the Retreat. He was raised among archivists and seed keepers, people who measure their work in centuries, and he entered the Nexus academy on its analysis track with no ambition to leave the archive at all. He was the first analyst in a decade to request transfer to field duty, and the request was granted only after two appeals.

His specialty is attestation. Eklund can reconstruct an event's true sequence from fragments that other agents would dismiss as noise, and in the field he confirms the facts of a scene faster than almost anyone in the Corps. His jump efficiency is poor, however, and his handlers budget extra chronal for every deployment he makes.

The red ocular implant over his right eye is a period-spectrum analyzer of his own specification. It reads ink, paper, fabric and metal and dates them at a glance, and it is the reason he is sent to events where the documentary record has been deliberately altered. Period witnesses assume it is a medical patch. He has never corrected them.

Eklund is soft-spoken, precise and entirely unsentimental about the past. He regards history as a set of claims to be tested, and he is uncomfortable with agents who treat it as anything else. Colleagues say he is the person to ask what actually happened, and the last person to ask whether it ought to have.
