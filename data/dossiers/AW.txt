WREN, Aislin Maeve — Field Agent, Survey Division (ID: AW)

Wren was born in 2152 in the Dublin Uplands and is the youngest agent on the active roster. She was a prodigy, entering the Nexus academy at seventeen and graduating at nineteen with the highest composite score in a decade. She is now twenty-two, and her superiors are still deciding what to make of her.

She is assigned to Survey Division, where new agents learn the field by mapping events without changing them. Wren has excelled at it. Her chronal efficiency and attestation scores are both well above average, and her survey reports are thorough, insightful and occasionally more opinionated than Survey protocol allows.

Wren is quiet and serious on assignment, dressed in practical field armor under her period clothing, which she insists on wearing even for routine surveys. She keeps her eyes down and her ears open, and her handlers say she notices things that agents with twenty years' experience walk straight past.

She is ambitious, earnest and impatient to be given real responsibility. She has applied for transfer to Intervention Division twice, and been told both times that she is not ready. Command's private assessment is that she will be, and soon, and that the Corps should be very careful what it teaches her in the meantime.
