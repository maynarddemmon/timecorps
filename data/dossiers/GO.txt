GORDON, Lucian Mercer — Field Agent, Time Corps (ID: GO)

Gordon was born in 2135 in the Geneva Arcology, the only child of two diplomats in the post-Retreat Accord service. He spent his childhood in embassies and negotiating rooms, learning to read a room long before he learned to read a map. He came to the Corps late, at twenty-eight, after a short and spectacular career in treaty mediation ended when he quietly declined to endorse a settlement he believed would fail within a decade. It failed in six years. The Corps recruited him the following spring.

His academy file is unusual. His instructors rated his technical fundamentals as merely adequate, but his assessment of human behavior on site was near perfect. He could walk into an unfamiliar event and within minutes identify who held authority, who resented it, and which conversation would change the outcome. The academy director wrote that Gordon "does not alter history so much as persuade it." He was assigned to Intervention Division, the section that handles events decided by people rather than machinery.

Gordon cultivates an appearance that colleagues find either charming or exhausting: tinted lenses in every era, a fresh flower at his lapel, and a cigarette he rarely lights. The affectations are deliberate. He argues that a memorable manner makes people remember the manner instead of the man, and his deployment record supports him: in eleven insertions, no contemporary witness has ever described his face accurately. He is less careful with paradox than his superiors would like, and his reports sometimes list a conversation's result without explaining how he got it.<!--

After the Titanic rescue, Command released Gordon from Intervention Division for assignment to the current team. His transfer notes observe that the next phase of operations will depend less on what ships do than on what their captains, owners and admiralties decide, which is exactly the kind of problem Gordon was recruited to solve.-->
