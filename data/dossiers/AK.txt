KASLIWAL, Arjun Dev — Field Agent, Intervention Division (ID: AK)

Kasliwal was born in 2137 in the Mumbai Stilts, the elevated city built above the flooded harbor during the Retreat. His family ran a trading house that dealt in everything from salvaged machinery to rumors, and he spent his youth brokering deals between people who did not trust each other. He was recruited at twenty-six after a Corps officer watched him settle a salvage-rights dispute between three rival crews in an afternoon.

He joined Intervention Division, where his instinct for leverage made him a natural. Kasliwal understands that most historical decisions are made in back rooms by tired people, and that the right word at the right moment can change more than any amount of force. He is a strong investigator and an adequate navigator, and he is at his best in events decided around a table.

On assignment he dresses like a man of means and smokes like one, a habit he adopted early to give himself an excuse to linger in any conversation. His handlers have learned that when Kasliwal lights a cigarette in the field, it means he has found the person he was looking for and is deciding what to say.

He is genial, patient and far more calculating than he appears. Colleagues enjoy his company and do not play cards with him. His mission reports are unusually candid about his own mistakes, which he says is the only way to make sure he never repeats one.
