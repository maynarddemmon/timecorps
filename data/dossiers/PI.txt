PIERCE, Adrian Kessel — Chronal Systems Technician, Nexus Operations (ID: PI)

Pierce is not, by training, a field agent. Born in 2141 in the orbital habitat Meridian Ring, he came to the Corps as an engineer and spent eight years in Nexus Operations maintaining the jump arrays that send agents into the past. He knows the machinery of time travel better than almost anyone who uses it. He can tell from the pitch of an array's hum whether a deployment will land clean, and he has twice refused to send an agent through a jump window he did not trust. Both times the later analysis showed he was right.

His right forearm and hand are cybernetic, the result of a 2168 array failure in which a collapsing chronal field sheared away everything past the elbow in a fraction of a second. He was back in the array bay four months later. Coworkers say he talks to the replacement hand the way other people talk to a stubborn tool, and that the fine-control calibrations on it are his own work, far better than the stock unit the medical wing fitted.

Pierce has applied for field certification three times and been turned down each time, not for lack of ability but because Operations considered him too valuable to lose. His psychological file describes him as dry, blunt and deeply uncomfortable with the idea of changing events he can observe only through instruments. He once wrote that watching history from the array room was "like listening to a surgery through a wall."
