VARGA, Asha Lenke — Chronal Navigator, Nexus Operations (ID: AV)

Varga was born in 2143 in the Budapest Heights, the city that moved uphill from the flooded Danube. She trained in meditation and biofeedback before she ever encountered the Corps, and worked as a clinical therapist helping people recover from traumatic displacement. Nexus Operations recruited her after research showed that chronal navigators with meditative training made fewer errors in the most unstable regions of the field.

She is a navigator of an unusual kind. Varga does not compute jump solutions so much as feel them, guiding agents through the chronal field by instinct as much as instruments. Her chronal efficiency is exceptional, and she was certified for field duty after her solutions proved consistently better when she carried them herself. She is a capable investigator, if a slow one.

On deployment she is still and composed to a degree that period witnesses find calming. She often closes her eyes when she is listening to the chronal field. Colleagues say they have seen her do it in the middle of a riot, and that she opened them again at exactly the right moment.

Varga is gentle, patient and difficult to fluster. She is the agent other navigators consult when a jump solution feels wrong, and she has a quiet authority in the Nexus that has nothing to do with rank. She believes that the chronal field has moods, and she has stopped trying to persuade her colleagues that she is serious.
