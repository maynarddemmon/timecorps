UEDA, Aurora Kei — Field Agent, Containment Division (ID: AU)

Ueda was born in 2146 in the San Francisco Bayshelf, the city rebuilt on the terraced hills above the drowned bay. She served five years with the Bayshelf Emergency Corps, responding to the landslides and seawall breaches that still threaten the city, before the Time Corps recruited her into Containment Division.

She is a specialist in unstable events: situations where paradox has begun to spread and period witnesses are becoming aware that something is wrong. Ueda is fast, precise and calm under pressure, and she has a reputation for stabilizing events that other agents had written off. Her chronal efficiency is well above average, and her investigative work is sound if not exceptional.

She carries a period sidearm on every deployment and has used it more often than most agents in the division. Her after-action reports are clinical, and she has never been found at fault in any of the incidents they describe. She prefers light armor under period clothing, and considers the Corps' standard field kit dangerously flimsy.

Ueda is intense, focused and difficult to know. She trains relentlessly between deployments and expects the same of her partners. Colleagues who have worked with her say she is the agent you want beside you when an event starts to come apart, and the one who will tell you, in detail, exactly what you did wrong afterwards.
