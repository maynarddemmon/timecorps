IONESCU, Bella Silvia — Paradox Theorist, Nexus Operations (ID: BI)

Ionescu was born in 2140 in the Bucharest Heights and trained as a theoretical physicist. She spent eight years at the Geneva Arcology developing the mathematical models the Corps uses to predict paradox, and she joined Nexus Operations at thirty to refine them against real deployments. She was certified for field duty after arguing, successfully, that she could not understand paradox without experiencing it.

She deploys to measure paradox in the field, recording how it forms, spreads and dissipates. Ionescu's chronal efficiency is high and her attestation work sound, and her field measurements have transformed the Corps' understanding of how events react to intervention. The paradox load on her file is the result of deliberate exposure, and the medical wing has twice refused her requests to increase it.

The red markings beneath her eyes are chronal burns from an early measurement, which she regards as a mark of her profession. On deployment she moves carefully, observing everything, and she has a habit of pausing mid-sentence to note down something only she has noticed.

Ionescu is brilliant, intense and impatient with anyone who treats paradox as a simple penalty. She believes that paradox is the timeline's way of healing itself, and that the Corps understands it far less well than it thinks. Her colleagues find her lectures fascinating and her deployments alarming.
