DUNCAN, Amon Thaddeus — Field Agent, Recovery Division (ID: AD)

Duncan was born in 2138 in the orbital habitat Halcyon Wheel, where his parents maintained the station's water reclamation plant. He came down to Earth at eighteen to join the Continental Rescue Service, spending six years pulling survivors out of flood collapses in the old European lowlands. The Corps found him through that service. Recovery Division needs agents who go toward disasters rather than away from them, and Duncan had never learned to do anything else.

Recovery agents extract people: stranded Corps personnel, compromised deep placements, and occasionally historical figures whose survival an operation depends on. Duncan's jump efficiency is among the highest in the division, and he regularly reaches a site with chronal to spare for a second attempt if the first fails. What he lacks is patience for investigation. His reports are clear about who he brought back and much vaguer about what was happening when he arrived.

The augmented eye on the left side of his face replaced one lost to a temporal shear during an extraction from Pompeii in 2167, when the event boundary moved faster than the models predicted. It reads chronal gradients directly, a modification he designed with the medical wing and which is now being trialed across the division.

Duncan is warm, blunt and relentlessly practical. He thinks of every mission in terms of who needs to be carried out and how long he has to do it, and he tends to treat everything else as somebody else's problem.
