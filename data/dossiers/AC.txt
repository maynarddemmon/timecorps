CROWN, Aaron Blackwood — Senior Field Agent, Time Corps (ID: AC)

Crown was born in 2122 in the Edinburgh Enclave and is one of the longest-serving field agents on the active roster. He joined the Corps in its first decade, when jump arrays were unstable and procedures were written after the fact from whatever had gone wrong. Several of the rules new agents learn at the academy exist because Crown broke them first and survived.

His record includes more than sixty insertions, two formal reprimands and three commendations, one of them for recovering an entire survey team from a collapsing event in the Thirty Years' War. He was among the first agents to witness a Chronovore manifestation and report it accurately. The paradox load on his file is the accumulated cost of a career spent in the years before the Corps understood how paradox worked.

Crown has been offered command positions many times and has turned all of them down. He says he is too old to learn to send other people where he would rather go himself. Command has quietly limited him to lower-risk assignments, which he accepts with poor grace and occasionally ignores.

He is dour, dry and impatient with ceremony, and his reports are notoriously blunt. Younger agents find him intimidating until they are deployed alongside him, after which most of them ask to be deployed alongside him again. He keeps his own counsel about the events he has changed, and he has said only once, in a debrief transcript that is now required reading at the academy, that he does not believe any of them turned out better for it.
