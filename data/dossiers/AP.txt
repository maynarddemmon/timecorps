PASCAL, Aurelien Marc — Field Agent, Recovery Division (ID: AP)

Pascal was born in 2144 in Marseille Haute, the upper city built above the flooded harbor. He grew up in the casinos and card rooms of the reconstruction years, where his father dealt cards and his mother ran the books, and he learned to read odds before he learned arithmetic. He came to the Corps at twenty-five after a brief and lucrative career as a professional gambler, which ended when the Corps offered him a game with higher stakes.

In Recovery Division he has become known for fast, improbable extractions. His chronal efficiency is exceptional. He jumps in, gets out and treats the whole operation as a calculated risk rather than a rescue. His investigative work is weak, and his reports are often a single paragraph describing what he did with no account of why.

Pascal is relaxed to the point of insolence. He lounges in period furniture, wears dark glasses indoors and gives every impression of being bored by events that would terrify most agents. Colleagues say the manner is genuine, and that he simply does not believe anything will go wrong until it does, at which point he becomes extremely focused.

He is charming, reckless and unexpectedly kind to the people he recovers. Command has reprimanded him twice for taking unnecessary risks and commended him four times for the results. His handlers keep a quiet wager on how long his luck will hold, and he has asked to be told the odds.
