BRANDT, Bianca Liesel — Signals Specialist, Intervention Division (ID: BB)

Brandt was born in 2147 in the Berlin Commons and trained as a communications engineer, specializing in the restoration of pre-Retreat radio networks. She spent five years rebuilding the emergency broadcast system across central Europe before the Corps recruited her for her encyclopedic knowledge of early wireless technology.

She is Intervention Division's specialist in communications history: telegraphs, early wireless sets, signal stations and the operators who ran them. Brandt can operate every Marconi set built between 1900 and 1920 and send Morse at a speed that impresses period operators. Her attestation work is strong, particularly in events where messages were sent, received or lost.

The device fitted over the left side of her head is a signal receiver of her own design, tuned to period wireless frequencies. It lets her hear transmissions as they happen and, if necessary, interrupt them. Period witnesses take it for a hearing aid, and she has learned to cup her ear convincingly when they speak to her.

Brandt is sharp, sardonic and completely absorbed in her work. She keeps a log of every transmission she has intercepted in the past, and she has an unsettling habit of quoting historical telegrams from memory in ordinary conversation. Her colleagues say she can tell you what any ship at sea was saying on any night in history, and they are only partly joking.
