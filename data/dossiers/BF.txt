FALK, Bodil Ingrid — Marksman, Containment Division (ID: BF)

Falk was born in 2139 in the Uppsala Forest Settlements, where her family were foresters and hunters. She served eight years in the Nordic Border Guard as a marksman and patrol leader before joining the Corps at twenty-nine. Containment Division recruited her for her discipline, her patience and her extraordinary accuracy.

She is the division's only marksman, and she is deployed rarely. Falk's role is to protect other agents during the most dangerous containments, watching from a distance and intervening only when an event is about to turn lethal. She has fired her weapon in the past on four occasions, all of them recorded in detail and all of them cleared by review. Her chronal efficiency is high and her investigative work weak.

On deployment she carries a period rifle and a great deal of patience. She can wait motionless for hours, and period witnesses almost never notice her. She says the art of her work is not in shooting but in waiting, and that she has spent a hundred hours watching for every second she has spent acting.

Falk is quiet, reserved and entirely self-possessed. She rarely speaks during missions and says little more between them. Her colleagues trust her absolutely, and she has made it clear that she would rather never fire again than fire once when she did not need to.
