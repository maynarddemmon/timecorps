MENSAH, Amadou Kwabena — Quartermaster Agent, Deep Placement (ID: AM)

Mensah was born in 2133 in Accra Highcity, the relocated capital built on the ridge above the old coast. He ran a supply business that outfitted salvage expeditions along the drowned West African shoreline, and he knows more about what people need to survive in unfamiliar places than almost anyone in the Corps. Deep Placement recruited him at thirty-one to solve a problem it had never solved well: keeping long-term agents equipped, paid and plausible in the past.

He became the division's quartermaster. Mensah plans the money, documents, clothing and cover stories that sustain agents through placements lasting months, and he occasionally deploys himself to deliver them. His investigative instincts are strong, and his handlers value his reports on the economies of the periods he visits more than he does.

He is a large man with a fondness for bright tailoring, and he wears tinted glasses in every era because, he says, sensible people should never let a trader see where they are looking. He is scrupulous with the Corps' money and extravagant with his own, and his period expense reports are widely regarded as works of fiction that nobody has been able to disprove.

Mensah is warm, shrewd and endlessly patient with agents who come to him in trouble. He has personally arranged the recovery of three stranded deep placements, and he keeps a standing file of provisions ready for any agent listed as unaccounted for, just in case they turn up needing a coat, a ticket and a name.
