OKONJO, Adaeze Mira — Field Agent (Stranded), Time Corps (ID: OK)

Okonjo was born in 2143 in the Lagos Terraces, the stepped seawall city built when the old coastline was surrendered during the Retreat. Her mother ran a tide-power cooperative and her father taught history at the terrace school, and she grew up convinced that both jobs were about the same thing: holding back something much larger than yourself. She entered the Nexus academy at nineteen and graduated second in her cohort, first in field endurance.

Her record is defined by a single deployment. In 2171 she was inserted into an event cluster with a planned recall window of six hours. A jump array fault at the Nexus closed the window early, and her return signal was never received. Her chronal reserves, already low from an unscheduled second jump, ran dry trying to force a recall on her own. She has been stranded ever since, with the scar at her hairline the only injury recorded in the fragmentary telemetry that still reaches HQ.

Stranded agents are not lost agents, and Okonjo's file is marked accordingly. The small paradox load registered against her suggests that she has been careful, living quietly inside a period that is not her own and avoiding every event she has already touched. Command believes she is waiting for the Corps to come back for her, and that she has not let herself hope too much that it will.

Instructors remember Okonjo as calm to the point of stillness, and impossible to rattle. Her academy mentor wrote that she "treats panic as a resource to be spent later, if at all." Any agent who locates her should expect to find her exactly where the evidence says she will be, with a clear report ready and very little patience for sympathy.
