JAEGER, Brynn Solveig — Test Agent, Nexus Operations (ID: BJ)

Jaeger was born in 2146 in the Bergen Fjordlands and trained as a test pilot, flying experimental aircraft for the Nordic aerospace consortium. She joined the Corps at twenty-six, recruited by Nexus Operations to test new jump equipment in the field before it is issued to other agents.

The device she wears over her head is a prototype portable jump rig, designed to let agents jump without returning to the Nexus arrays. It is heavy, temperamental and still highly experimental, and Jaeger is the only agent certified to use it. Her chronal efficiency is the highest ever recorded, largely because of the rig. Her investigative work is poor, and she spends most of her deployments monitoring her equipment rather than her surroundings.

Period witnesses find the rig impossible to explain, and Jaeger rarely tries. She deploys to remote events and brief windows where she is unlikely to be seen, and she has a reputation for arriving somewhere improbable and leaving before anyone can ask questions.

Jaeger is bold, cheerful and completely unafraid of equipment failure. She has survived three rig malfunctions, one of which stranded her for nine hours in 1720, and she regards each one as valuable data. Her colleagues admire her courage and question her judgment, often in the same sentence.
