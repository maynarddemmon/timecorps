AIMES, Alexander Rowe — Field Agent, Intervention Division (ID: AA)

Aimes was born in 2146 in the Montreal Undercity, the climate-sealed districts built beneath the old downtown after the long winters of the Retreat. He was the youngest of five in a family of courier pilots, and he learned early that arriving on time and arriving unnoticed were two different skills. He mastered both. A Corps talent scout found him running encrypted medical cargo across the Northern Reach at twenty-two and offered him a place at the Nexus academy on the spot.

At the academy he was an average student with one exceptional quality: he could blend into any crowd in any period after a single briefing. Instructors ran him through dozens of simulated insertions, and in none of them did a period witness single him out. His evaluators recommended him for Intervention Division, where agents work through conversations and small nudges rather than direct action.

Aimes favors sharp tailoring and dark glasses on assignment, and argues that looking like you belong somewhere is most of the work of belonging there. His deployments are brief and efficient. He prefers to arrive, change one thing, and leave before anyone can ask who he is, and his reports are among the shortest in the division.

Colleagues describe him as easy company and hard to read. He is fiercely loyal to the courier habits of his childhood: he never carries more than he needs, he never takes the same route twice, and he never leaves a message behind unless someone is meant to find it.
