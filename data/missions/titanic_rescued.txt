OPERATION TITANIC — Passengers Rescued

The Corps' analysts have concluded that the Titanic must sink. The disaster drove the first international rules for safety at sea, and a history without it is a history in which the next great liner sails just as unprepared. The loss of life, however, is not required.

Your objectives are to let the collision happen as history records, and to ensure that the passengers survive it.

The Titanic carried twenty lifeboats, with room for fewer than 1,200 of the more than 2,200 people aboard, although her davits were designed to carry far more. Some miles to the north, the Californian lay stopped in the ice. Her only wireless operator had turned in for the night, and she never heard the Titanic's call for help.

Agents may be needed far from the night of the sinking itself. Decisions made in the drafting offices of Harland & Wolff, and in the wireless room of a ship that never knew she was needed, may matter more than anything done on the boat deck.
