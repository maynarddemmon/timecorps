OLD HEAD OF KINSALE — County Cork, Ireland

The Old Head of Kinsale is a long, narrow headland pushing south into the Atlantic from the coast of County Cork, crowned by a lighthouse. It is one of the great landmarks of the Irish coast for ships making for Liverpool, and captains steer for it to fix their position before running up St George's Channel.

That makes the waters off it a natural hunting ground. A submarine waiting here can expect ships to come to it, passing close to the headland on predictable courses.

The sea eleven miles south of the Old Head is a little over 300 feet deep. The seabed there is where the Lusitania will lie, on her starboard side, for more than a century.

OPERATIVE NOTES: The headland is visible from far out to sea in clear weather, and from the lighthouse the view to the south is wide. Fishing boats from Kinsale work these waters and are often the first vessels on any scene.
