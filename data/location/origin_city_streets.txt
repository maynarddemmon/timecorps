A small river city that used to make things. The mills closed a generation ago, and the warehouse district they left behind is half lofts and half for lease. Downtown is six blocks of brick storefronts, a courthouse, two churches and a bank that’s a pharmacy now. The Ledger still lands on doorsteps every morning, and the Orpheum still lights its marquee every night, whether or not anyone is coming.

Nobody drives downtown if they can help it. The number 9 bus crosses the city from the river to the warehouses and back, and most of the city rides it. It rains more often than it doesn’t, and people talk about the weather the way they talk about a difficult relative.
