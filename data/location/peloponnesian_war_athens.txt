ATHENS — Attica, Greece
<p>At the height of its power, a city of perhaps a quarter of a million people, joined to its harbor at the Piraeus by the Long Walls, four miles of fortified road. The Parthenon is finished; tribute from some 150 cities pays for the fleet.
<p>Every male citizen may speak and vote in the assembly on the Pnyx, and juries of hundreds of citizens try every case. The orators who move the crowd rule Athens, until the crowd changes its mind.
<p>In 430 BCE a plague kills perhaps a quarter of the people crowded inside the walls, Pericles among them.
<p>OPERATIVE NOTES: Athens is full of foreigners, metics and slaves, so a stranger passes easily in the streets and the marketplace. Only citizens may speak in the assembly, but anyone can talk to a citizen.
