Corps physicists call it a place because their instruments need somewhere to point. It isn’t one. The Etherium is the layer of reality that decides how events may follow one another: which way cause runs, what is remembered as consequence and what as origin. It doesn’t make time. It decides how time should behave.

Every jump crosses it, briefly, and chronal is the price of the crossing. Agents who come back describe corridors and thresholds, which the oldest commentaries call a mistake born of proximity. The Etherium is not a network of roads. It is the rule that decides which roads may exist at all, and whether they run downhill or up.

Push against it too often and it pushes back, not as punishment but as correction. The Corps’ word for the strain is paradox. Its word for the correction is chronovores.
