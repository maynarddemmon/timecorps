THE HELLESPONT AND IONIA — Western Asia Minor
<p>The Greek cities of the Asian coast, Miletus, Ephesus and the rest, and the narrow strait of the Hellespont that joins the Aegean to the Sea of Marmara. Athens imports the grain that feeds its people through the Hellespont from the Black Sea; whoever closes it can starve Athens.
<p>Behind the coast lie the Persian satraps, at Sardis and Dascylium, who have never given up their claim to the Greek cities, and who would pay well to see Athens humbled.
<p>Aegospotami, “Goat Rivers”, is an open beach on the European shore of the Hellespont, with no harbor and no town to feed a fleet.
<p>OPERATIVE NOTES: The coast is a patchwork of cities that change sides with the war. Mercenaries and exiles are everywhere, and gold buys almost anything.
