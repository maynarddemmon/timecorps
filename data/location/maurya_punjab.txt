TAXILA AND THE INDUS — Northwest India
<p>Taxila, between the Indus and the Jhelum, is a city of scholars, where students from all over India come to study medicine, law and the arts of war. Tradition says Chanakya taught here.
<p>West of the Indus lie the passes to Afghanistan and Persia, and the road every invader takes. Alexander came this way, and Seleucus will.
<p>After Alexander, Greek governors and garrisons hold parts of the land, and Indian kings hold the rest, each waiting to see who will rise.
<p>OPERATIVE NOTES: Traders, students and soldiers of every people pass through Taxila. A foreign scholar fits in well; a foreign soldier, less so.
