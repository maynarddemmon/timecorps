PURSER'S OFFICE — C Deck, RMS Titanic

The purser's enquiry office stands on C deck beside the forward first-class entrance and its grand staircase. It is the ship's front desk: passengers come here to lodge valuables in the safe, change cabins, change money, complain, and send telegrams.

Chief Purser Hugh McElroy, a tall and popular officer, runs the office with a staff of clerks. Messages handed in at the counter, charged at twelve shillings and sixpence for the first ten words, are sent up to the Marconi room by pneumatic tube. On a busy day the tube barely stops.

The office handles the ship's papers as well as its passengers. Anything lost, left behind, or unclaimed after the shuffle at Southampton tends to end up in one of its drawers.

OPERATIVE NOTES: The counter is public to first-class passengers; the rooms behind it are not. The clerks are busy and trusting with anyone in a purser's uniform. The pneumatic tube to the wireless room is a quiet route for a message that must not be seen being carried.
