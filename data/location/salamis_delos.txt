DELOS — Cyclades, Aegean Sea
<p>A small, rocky island at the center of the Cyclades, sacred as the birthplace of Apollo and Artemis. Later, Athens will purify it, and forbid anyone to be born or die there.
<p>The sanctuary of Apollo, with its sacred lake and its terrace of marble lions, draws pilgrims and festivals from all over the Ionian world.
<p>Neutral, holy and central, it is the natural place for the cities of the Aegean to swear an alliance and keep their common treasury.
<p>OPERATIVE NOTES: Pilgrims, envoys and traders come and go all the time, and no one questions a stranger who has come to sacrifice. The treasury in the temple is another matter.
