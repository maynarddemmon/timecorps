THE PUNJAB — Northwest India
<p>The land of the five rivers that flow into the Indus, rich, crowded and divided among kings and republics. Persia ruled the Indus valley for two centuries, but its hold has long since slipped.
<p>Alexander crosses the Indus in 326 BCE, defeats King Porus at the Hydaspes, and makes him an ally. Beyond the Hyphasis, the last river, lie the Ganges and the kingdom of the Nanda, with an army said to number two hundred thousand.
<p>The monsoon has fallen on the army for seventy days. The soldiers are tired of rain, snakes and war.
<p>OPERATIVE NOTES: Greeks are a novelty and a terror here. Interpreters are scarce, so a stranger who speaks both Greek and an Indian language will find work, and attention.
