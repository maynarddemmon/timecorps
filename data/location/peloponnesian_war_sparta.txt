SPARTA — Laconia, Peloponnese, Greece
<p>Five unwalled villages on the Eurotas River, ruling the southern Peloponnese and leading a league of allied cities. Two kings, five ephors elected each year, a council of elders and an assembly of all Spartan citizens.
<p>The Spartans are few, a few thousand men and fewer every generation, and every one is a soldier. They live off the labor of the helots, whom they fear far more than any foreign enemy.
<p>Sparta has no fleet worth the name and no money to build one. To fight Athens at sea, it will need someone else’s gold.
<p>OPERATIVE NOTES: Strangers are not welcome, and are expelled from time to time. Envoys from allied cities are heard, and the assembly decides by shouting.
