PATALIPUTRA — Magadha, the Ganges plain, India
<p>The capital of Magadha, the richest kingdom of the Ganges, where the Son River meets the Ganges. Its timber walls run some nine miles, with hundreds of towers and a moat.
<p>The Nanda kings rule it with a vast army and a full treasury, and they are hated: for their taxes, for their greed, and because the first of them is said to have been the son of a barber.
<p>Magadha has been the cradle of new teachings: the Buddha and Mahavira both taught nearby, two centuries ago.
<p>OPERATIVE NOTES: The court is full of Brahmins, merchants, spies and petitioners. A wandering ascetic or a scholar can go almost anywhere, and is listened to.
