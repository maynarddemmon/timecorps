BABYLON — Mesopotamia
<p>The greatest city of the east, on the Euphrates, with double walls wide enough for a chariot to turn on top, the Ishtar Gate and the temple tower of Marduk. Its astronomers have kept a nightly record of the sky for centuries.
<p>Babylon has belonged to Persia since Cyrus took it in 539 BCE. Its priests and nobles have no great love for Persian kings, and may welcome a new one who honors their gods.
<p>Alexander will make it his capital, and die here, in Nebuchadnezzar’s palace, in June of 323 BCE.
<p>OPERATIVE NOTES: A cosmopolitan city of many languages, where Aramaic is spoken everywhere. Visitors are many; the temples and the palace are closely kept.
