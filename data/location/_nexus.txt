The Nexus isn’t a city, though it has streets, nor is it a single moment, though everyone who has passed within its halls speaks about it as one. It is, at least in part, the stretch of the twenty-second century in which the corridors of time became reachable. The threads of causality can be examined from here. Those with sufficient knowledge, may even alter their weave, thus shaping reality itself.

Every time traveller departs from the Nexus, and every recall signal points back to it. The Time Corps keeps its headquarters here in the Corps’ own year, 2174, tapped into the Pool of Chronal “Aether” that powers its agents. The Corps is not the only tenant. Earlier in the reckoning of the Nexus, somewhere among the shuttered freight yards, the Syndicate keeps a warehouse the Corps has never found.
