SPARTA — Laconia, Peloponnese, Greece
<p>Not so much a city as five villages without walls, on the bank of the Eurotas in the south of the Peloponnese, some 150 miles from Athens by road. Sparta needs no walls: its army is the best in Greece, and every Spartan citizen is a soldier from boyhood.
<p>Two kings rule together, with a council of elders and five ephors elected each year. The Spartans are few, and they live on the labor of the helots, a conquered people who greatly outnumber them.
<p>Sparta is devout, and slow to march. It keeps its religious laws even in war.
<p>OPERATIVE NOTES: Spartans distrust foreigners and expel them from time to time. A messenger from an allied city will be heard by the ephors; anyone else will be watched, and probably sent away.
