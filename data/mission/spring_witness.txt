SPRING RETURNS — WITNESS

Shanghai, 12 November 2081.

The device has brought you to a crowded hall on the Huangpu waterfront, where a scientist is about to tell the world that growing old is now optional.

The old stranger's message was clear: watch, and learn how this came about. Don't touch anything, not yet.

Watch the announcement. Listen, read, ask questions. The more you learn here, the more the device will show you of the events that led to it: who made the breakthrough, who paid for it, and who decided the world should know.
