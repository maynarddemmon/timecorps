SPRING RETURNS — THE VOTE

Beijing, 22 September 2087.

The woman waiting for you outside the Expo hall wears a red visor pushed up into her hair, and a Chronofex like yours. "Beniko Asano," she says. "Time Corps. That krono on your wrist belonged to one of my people, and it's been screaming at us for a week."

The announcement you just watched shouldn't have happened, she tells you. Not yet, and not like this. When the Commission met in September, the vote went the other way: the world was told about the treatment's lesser benefits, and the reversal stayed a secret. "Not fair," she says. "Not what I'd have chosen. But it's the history we have, and someone has been rewriting it."

Someone reached three of the commissioners in the weeks before the vote. Find out who, and change their minds. Two votes is all it takes.

She tops up your krono from her own, and unlocks enough of it for you to make the jumps. "Don't waste it," she says. "And don't let them see you coming."
