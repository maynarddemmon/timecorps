In the late summer of 490 BCE, a Persian fleet under Datis and Artaphernes crossed the Aegean to punish Athens for helping the Ionian revolt. Guided by the exiled tyrant Hippias, the army landed in the bay of Marathon, a day’s march from the city. The Athenians marched out to meet it with the men of Plataea, and on the morning of the battle they charged and drove the Persians into the sea.
<p>Your objective is to make sure that battle happens, and that Athens wins it.
<p>Analysts report that the generals’ vote has turned. Ten generals were split evenly on whether to attack or wait for Sparta, and the deciding vote belonged to the polemarch, Callimachus. In the record we now have, he votes to wait. While Athens waits, the Persians embark their cavalry for the city, and the friends of Hippias inside the walls get the time they need.
<p>Deploy to the landing, learn what you can about the camps, and find your way to the generals before they vote.
