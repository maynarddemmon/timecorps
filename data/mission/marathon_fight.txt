In the late summer of 490 BCE, a Persian fleet under Datis and Artaphernes crossed the Aegean to punish Athens for helping the Ionian revolt. Guided by the exiled tyrant Hippias, the army landed in the bay of Marathon, a day’s march from the city. Athens sent the runner Pheidippides to ask Sparta for help, marched out to meet the landing with the men of Plataea, and on the morning of the battle charged and drove the Persians into the sea. When the Persian fleet sailed around the cape to take the undefended city, it found the army already back, and turned for home.
<p>Your objective is to make sure Athens survives, to become the Athens of the golden age.
<p>Analysts report that the record has gone wrong in more than one place. No runner leaves for Sparta. The ten generals split evenly on whether to attack or wait for Sparta, and the polemarch, Callimachus, who has the deciding vote, votes to wait. While Athens waits, the Persians keep their cavalry on the beach, and the friends of Hippias inside the walls get the time they need. Athens falls.
<p>Deploy to the landing, learn what you can about the camps, and find out where the record can be put right.
