OPERATION LUSITANIA — Doesn't Sink

RMS Lusitania, the Cunard Line's express liner, left New York for Liverpool on 1 May 1915, with Britain and Germany at war and German submarines operating off the coast of Ireland. On the afternoon of 7 May, off the Old Head of Kinsale, she was struck by a single torpedo from U-20. A second explosion followed, and she sank in eighteen minutes. 1,198 people died.

Your objective is to keep the Lusitania afloat.

She was not unwarned. The German embassy published a warning in the New York papers on the morning she sailed, and the Admiralty sent signals about submarine activity in her path. Even so, she approached Ireland at reduced speed, with one of her boiler rooms shut down to save coal and labour, and her captain held a steady course to fix his position at exactly the moment U-20 was waiting.

Technician Pierce joins the team for this operation. His understanding of chronal behaviour may prove as valuable as any agent's skill in the field.
