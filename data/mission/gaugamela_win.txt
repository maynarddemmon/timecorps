In the autumn of 331 BCE, Alexander of Macedon crossed the Tigris to meet Darius III and the whole strength of the Persian Empire. An eclipse of the moon was read as an omen of Persian defeat. Alexander refused to attack at night, and on the morning of 1 October, on the plain of Gaugamela, he opened the Persian line and charged at Darius, who fled. Babylon opened its gates, and Persia was Alexander’s.
<p>Your objective is to make sure Alexander wins, and Babylon falls.
