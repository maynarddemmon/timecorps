In 480 BCE, ten years after Marathon, Xerxes himself came to Greece with the largest army the world had seen and a fleet to match. After Thermopylae, Athens was emptied and burned. The Greek fleet, more than half of it Athenian, waited at Salamis, where Themistocles kept the quarreling admirals together and tricked Xerxes into attacking in the narrows. The Persian fleet was broken, and Xerxes went home.
<p>Your objective is to make sure Xerxes goes home.
<p>Analysts report that the admirals’ council at Salamis votes to retreat to the Isthmus, where the fleet will break up city by city, and that no message reaches the Persians to draw them into the straits.
<p>Deploy to the fleet at Salamis, find out how Themistocles holds it together, and see that he succeeds.
