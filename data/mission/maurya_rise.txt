Around 322 BCE, a young man named Chandragupta, taught and guided by the Brahmin Chanakya, overthrew the hated Nanda kings of Magadha and took their capital, Pataliputra. Alexander’s withdrawal had left the northwest in confusion, and Chandragupta took that too. When Alexander’s general Seleucus came to win it back, he made peace instead, and took five hundred elephants home.
<p>Your objective is to make sure Chandragupta rises, and Seleucus makes peace.
<p>Analysts report that Chanakya never finds his pupil, and that Chandragupta never learns the lesson of the porridge: to take an empire from the edges in.
<p>The stories of Chanakya come from legends written down centuries later. Deploy to the Punjab as the Greeks leave, and find where they can be made true.
