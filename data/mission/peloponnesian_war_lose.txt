For twenty-seven years, from 431 to 404 BCE, Athens and its empire fought Sparta and its allies. Athens ruled the sea and Sparta the land, and neither could beat the other, until Athens sent its greatest fleet to Sicily and lost it, Persian gold paid for a Spartan navy, and Lysander caught the last Athenian fleet on the beach at Aegospotami. Athens surrendered, and its Long Walls came down.
<p>Your objective is to make sure Athens loses.
<p>This is a hard thing to protect. But a Greece left exhausted by this war is the Greece that Macedon will lead into Asia, and everything after depends on that.
<p>Analysts report that the record has gone wrong in three places: Athens stays home from Sicily, Persia keeps its gold, and the Athenian generals at Aegospotami listen to Alcibiades.
<p>Start in Sparta in 432 BCE, where the war begins, and find the places where Athens’ luck must run out.
